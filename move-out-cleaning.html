Move-out cleaning checklist

A complete final clean for the areas that usually matter most before handoff.

Rooms & Floors

  • Dust reachable surfaces and fixtures
  • Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors
  • Wipe baseboards and visible trim
  • Remove trash and final debris

Kitchen & Bathrooms

  • Clean sinks, counters and appliance exteriors
  • Clean cabinet fronts and reachable shelves
  • Sanitize toilets, tubs, showers and vanities
  • Polish mirrors and high-touch surfaces

Move-out cleaning FAQ

Can this help with a security deposit?

A detailed clean can help leave the property in better condition, but deposit decisions are made by the landlord or manager.

Should the property be empty?

Yes, an empty property allows the most complete move-out clean.

Do you clean inside appliances?

Interior appliance cleaning can be requested when you ask for a quote.

Do you clean rental apartments?

Yes, we clean apartments, homes, condos and rental properties.
