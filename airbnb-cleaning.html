Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist

Every property is different, but these are the core items hosts usually need between guest stays.

Living Areas & Bedrooms

  • Dust reachable surfaces and furniture
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Make beds and reset pillows
  • Remove trash and refresh presentation

Kitchen & Bathrooms

  • Clean counters, sinks and appliance exteriors
  • Sanitize toilets, tubs, showers and vanities
  • Restock visible essentials when provided
  • Final walkthrough for guest-ready details

Airbnb cleaning FAQ

Can you clean between guest check-out and check-in?

Yes, when the schedule allows. Share the timing in your quote request.

Do you restock supplies?

We can restock visible supplies you provide, such as toiletries, towels or basic guest items.

Do you handle laundry?

Ask about laundry needs when requesting a quote so we can confirm the best setup.

Can I book recurring turnovers?

Yes. Regular host cleaning can be arranged based on property volume and availability.
