Move-in cleaning checklist

We focus on the areas that matter most before you bring your belongings inside.

Home Interior

  • Dust reachable surfaces and fixtures
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Wipe baseboards and visible trim
  • Clean doors, handles and high-touch areas

Kitchen & Bathrooms

  • Clean counters, sinks and appliance exteriors
  • Clean cabinet fronts and reachable shelves
  • Sanitize showers, tubs, toilets and vanities
  • Remove visible dust and move-in residue
